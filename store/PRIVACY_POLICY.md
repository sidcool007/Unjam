## Summary
Unjam: The Last Slot ("the app") does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. It has no account, no
advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service, and no in-app purchases, and it works fully offline.

## What the app stores on your device
To let you continue playing, the app saves a small file in its private storage on your device containing: your level
progress and stars, your in-game coin balance, your sound/music/reduced-motion settings, whether you have seen the
tutorial, and (if you leave mid-level) the current board so you can resume. It contains no name, email, device
identifier, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. This file never leaves your device and
is not included in Android cloud backups. You can erase it at any time via *Settings → Reset all progress*, or by
clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

## Permissions
The app requests **no Android permissions** (no internet, storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts or notifications).

## Third parties
The app is built with the open-source Godot Engine (MIT licence). The app contains no advertising, analytics or
tracking software development kits (SDKs) and makes no network connections. Google Play itself may process data
about your installation according to Google's own privacy policy; that is outside the app.
