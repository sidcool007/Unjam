# Human playtest plan — levels 1–10

**Status: NOT RUN. No playtest data exists.** Recruitment of testers is an outstanding **owner task**.

## Who / how many
5–8 people who have never seen the game, ideally mixed ages and casual-game experience.
One at a time, on a real phone, in portrait, with the debug or release-candidate build. Observe silently;
do not explain the rules. Record screen+voice if they consent.

## Script (≈15 min)
1. "Please play as you normally would and say what you are thinking." Start on the main menu.
2. Let them play levels 1–10 (stop at 15 min). Note every hesitation >5 s, every wrong tap, every undo/restart.
3. Never answer rule questions; note them verbatim and what finally resolved them.

## Observe (per level)
Time to first move · wrong taps and their toasts (did the message explain it?) · undo/restart/hint use ·
quit points · frustration/delight comments.

## Questions afterwards (1–5 scale unless stated)
1. I understood the rules without help. 2. When a move failed I understood why.
3. Difficulty rose fairly (which level first felt unfair/too easy?). 4. Hints were helpful (or: did you use one, did it help?).
5. Anything confusing, annoying or broken? (free text) 6. Would you keep playing? Why / why not?
7. What did "Free slots" / "Last slot!" mean to you? 8. Were buttons easy to tap and text easy to read?
9. Audio: pleasant / annoying / didn't notice.

## Decision rules (set before testing)
* ≥2 of 5 testers stuck >60 s on the same level or rule → fix tutorial text / level order.
* Median rule-understanding <4 → rewrite level-1 coach/intro.
* Any crash or lost progress → blocker.
